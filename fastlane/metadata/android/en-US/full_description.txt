Jesus' Corner is your daily space with God — Bible reading, guided prayer and reflection, in a simple routine that fits your time.

HOW IT WORKS
Three simple steps, every day: prayer, reading and reflection. Your streak only counts when all three are done, and you track everything on a history calendar.

STRUCTURED BIBLE READING
Choose from 4 plans — Light, Standard, Intensive or Free (no set pace) — and read the whole Bible organized into 8 thematic blocks, from the Old to the New Testament. Every session brings the full Bible text plus historical and geographical context chapter by chapter, across all 66 books, along with a map, background facts and space for your own notes.

GUIDED PRAYER (THE ACTS METHOD)
Adoration, Confession, Thanksgiving and Supplication — with a timer that moves on its own from step to step, so your prayer has direction without turning into a routine on autopilot.

GUIDED REFLECTION
Close the day with a 3-step outline — Relive, Understand and Apply — to pause and think about what God spoke to you through today's reading.

IN-DEPTH STUDIES
Four complete thematic studies — the Pentateuch, the Chronology of the Bible, Psalms and God's Covenants — with historical, geographical and theological context across 43 sessions, each with reflection questions.

COMMUNITY
Add friends, create reading groups and start named challenges, like "Mark Marathon." See what your friends are up to and read the Bible together, even from a distance.

PROGRESS AND ACHIEVEMENTS
Stats by book, by block and for the whole Bible, levels and achievements to keep you motivated, and a consistency chart for your routine.

AVAILABLE IN ENGLISH AND PORTUGUESE
All content — including historical context and background facts — is available in both languages, switchable anytime from your profile.

START FOR FREE
The free plan already lets you read the whole Bible at any pace, do guided prayer and reflection, listen with your device's voice, and track your progress — no ads on any plan. When you want more, Premium unlocks natural voice, hands-free mode, the chronological plan, highlights, notes, achievements and Community; Premium + AI adds an AI reading assistant and plans/studies generated by topic. You choose the plan, and you can switch or cancel anytime.

The Bible tells the story of a God who loves, seeks and never gives up on His people. Jesus' Corner exists to bring you closer to that story, every day.
