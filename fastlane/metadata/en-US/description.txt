Jesus' Corner is your daily space with God — Bible reading, prayer, and reflection, in a simple routine that fits your time.

HOW IT WORKS
Three steps a day: prayer, reading, and reflection, each taking as long as you choose. Your goal is weekly, not daily: you pick which days you want to read, and missing a day resets nothing.

BIBLE READING AT YOUR PACE
Choose from 4 paces — Light, Standard, Intensive, or Free — and read the whole Bible in book order, in chronological order, or in the order you prefer. Every chapter comes with the full text (NLT in English, NVT in Portuguese), historical and geographical context, a map, and trivia, and you can listen to it read aloud.

GUIDED PRAYER (ACTS METHOD)
Adoration, Confession, Thanksgiving, and Supplication, with time for each step. In Supplication, your prayer requests and your group's show up so you can pray for them right there.

REFLECTION
Close the day by writing what God spoke to you through the reading — or by answering three questions about the passage: Recall, Understand, and Apply.

ASK ABOUT THE TEXT
Stuck on a verse? Select the passage and ask. The answer always cites the Bible text behind it.

STUDIES
Four complete studies — Pentateuch, Bible Chronology, Psalms, and The Covenants of God — with historical, geographical, and theological context across 43 sessions. Plus topical studies and plans, built from the subject you choose.

LIBRARY
Highlights, verse notes, sermon notes, and reflections, all in one place and searchable.

PRAYER REQUESTS AND COMMUNITY
Share your requests, pray for your friends' and groups', and keep a record of how God answered. Create reading groups, read the same chapter together, and join challenges.

PROGRESS WITHOUT GUILT
The Bible wheel shows every book you've read. Earn a seal for each book you finish and achievements for consistency, and get a summary of your week every Sunday.

EVERY DAY ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
The verse of the day, your application phrase, and a reminder at the time you choose.

ENGLISH AND PORTUGUESE
All content in both languages, switchable anytime from your profile.

FREE OR PREMIUM
The free plan already lets you read the whole Bible at your pace, listen with your device's voice, write a reflection after each reading, get the verse of the day, and track your progress — no ads on any plan.
Premium, monthly or yearly, unlocks everything else: the guided daily plan with prayer, reading, and reflection in sequence, questions about the text, reflection questions, topical studies, the Library, Community with prayer requests, the natural voice with hands-free mode, and chronological order. Cancel anytime.

The Bible tells the story of a God who loves, pursues, and never gives up on His people. Jesus' Corner exists to bring you closer to that story every day.
